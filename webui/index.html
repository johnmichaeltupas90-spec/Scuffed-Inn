Booking a room

The list only shows rooms that are free for your dates.

  1. Pick your dates

    Check-in and check-out come first. Every room is checked against them.

  2. Choose a free room

    Only rooms open for those nights appear in the list, with their rate.

  3. Keep the number

    The confirmation carries a reservation number for check-in.
